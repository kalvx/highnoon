BEFORE YOU SADDLE UP

A few trail notes.

Can phone and PC players meet in Battles?

Yes. Use different accounts on the two updated devices, select roughly comparable teams, and enter Battles within the same minute. Both choices stay hidden until reveal. The connection search and each shared round allow 60 seconds.

Can I keep playing when the online service is unavailable?

Campaign and Practice are available offline. Campaign autosaves on your device and has its own collection, separate from your online Battles account.

How do I update without losing my Campaign?

Run the new Windows installer over your existing installation. On Android, open the new APK and choose Update. Keep the app installed and retain its storage so the device’s Campaign save stays with you.

Where can I get help or report a problem?

Join the High Noon Discord and include your game version, device and a short description of what happened.
